The problem

Her grades said she knew the material. Her scores said something else. That gap usually comes from test habits rather than ability: rushing the first module, choosing answers that are true but not supported by the passage, and losing track of what the question actually asks.

Most prep material didn't help. Paper books can't reproduce the digital test's adaptive structure, where the first module decides how hard the second one is. Free question banks give an answer key but rarely explain why the wrong answers were tempting.

Design decisions

REHEARSE THE REAL THING

Mirror the test exactly

Real module timing, adaptive routing to a standard or harder second module, and the same tools as the official app: flag for review, cross out choices, hide the timer.

TEACH THE PATTERN

Explain the trap, not just the answer

Every explanation names why the tempting wrong answer is wrong. The question types repeat, so learning the trap transfers to questions she hasn't seen.

DIAGNOSE THE HABIT

A mistake journal with reasons

Every miss is saved and tagged: misread, careless slip, trap answer, ran out of time. The journal surfaces the most common habit and the specific fix for it.

NEVER THE SAME TEST TWICE

Fresh questions every attempt

Math and grammar questions are generated with their answers and explanations computed by code, so they are always correct. Reading questions are drawn from a larger written pool.

What's inside

A full-length adaptive test with an estimated score and a breakdown by skill area. Untimed skill drills for each of the eight content domains. A strategy guide written for strong students who underperform on standardized tests. Progress saves automatically, and a test can be paused and resumed.

Results: the platform is in use now by the student it was built for. Her score change will be added here after her next official test.
