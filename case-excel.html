01

The problem

Finance and billing teams lose time on the same Excel tasks: matching records across reports, reconciling totals, summarizing by account at month-end.

Often they don't know what the function is called, so a search for "VLOOKUP" never happens. Generic help sites aren't written for AP, AR, payroll or close.

02

The learners

Accounting clerks, billing specialists and finance associates, often at nonprofits and healthcare organizations with little training budget.

They don't need a course. They need the answer while the spreadsheet is still open.

03

Design decisions

  • Chose performance support over a courseThe gap was finding the right method at the moment of need, not a lack of instruction. Knowing when not to build a course is a core design call.
    Needs analysis
  • Search in the learner's wordsType "pull vendor name from another sheet" or "numbers won't add up" and the tool returns the right function, shortcut or ready-made formula.
    Information design
  • Organized by accounting task, not Excel featureRecipes for AP, AR, GL and close, reconciliations, payroll, FP&A and audit, mapped to how finance work is actually done.
    Workflow analysis
  • Copy-ready answersFormulas copy in one tap, and a saved-favorites list keeps each person's go-to recipes close.
    Performance support
04

Results

The reference is live and free to use, with no account required. It's built from workflows used in revenue cycle, payroll and financial reporting at healthcare and human services organizations.

05

What's next

  • Add downloadable practice files so staff can try each recipe on sample data.
  • Pair the reference with a short live workshop for new finance hires.
