Excel for Accounting

The problem: Accounting teams lose time hunting for the right Excel function — often without knowing what it's called. Generic help sites aren't written for AP, AR, payroll or month-end close.

What we built: A plain-language search tool. Type "pull vendor name from another sheet" or "numbers won't add up" and it returns the right function, shortcut or ready-made formula.

  • 200+ functions, Windows and Mac shortcuts, features and recipes
  • Recipes for AP, AR, GL and close, reconciliations, payroll, FP&A and audit
  • Copy-ready formulas and a saved-favorites list

CompTIA Security+ Exam Prep

The problem: Security+ covers five broad domains, and many learners can't tell when they're actually ready for the exam.

What we built: A complete prep app that mirrors the real test: 90 questions weighted by domain, a 90-minute timer and flag-for-review.

  • Study notes for all five SY0-701 domains
  • Flashcards, a ports drill, and tap-to-order drills modeled on performance-based questions
  • Score breakdown by domain and a missed-question review queue

FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Prep

The problem: The FAA drone pilot exam now includes sectional chart images that aren't in the printed supplement, so memorizing figures no longer works.

What we built: A study app that teaches chart reading visually, with drawn chart symbols built right into the practice questions.

  • Chart Lab for airspace, obstacles, airport labels and elevation figures
  • Timed 60-question exams in the real format, plus topic drills with explanations
  • Flashcards and a missed-question review queue
