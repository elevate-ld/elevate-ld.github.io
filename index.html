Adaptive assessment · Test prep

Adaptive SAT & PSAT Practice

The problem: Strong students often score below their grades on the digital SAT and PSAT. Paper prep books can't copy the test's adaptive format, and most question banks don't explain why the wrong answers looked right.

What we built: A full-length practice test that adapts like the real exam: how you do on Module 1 decides whether Module 2 is the standard or harder version.

  • 98 questions across four timed modules, with an estimated score and a skill-area breakdown
  • Fresh math and grammar questions generated every attempt, plus a large written reading bank
  • Explanations that name the trap answer, and a mistake journal that shows which habits cost points
