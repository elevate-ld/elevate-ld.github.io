01

The problem

To fly drones commercially, pilots must pass the FAA Part 107 knowledge test. The material is spread across federal regulations, sectional charts, weather reports and FAA handbooks.

The test now includes chart images that aren't in the printed supplement. Learners who memorized figures from question dumps walk in unprepared for the skill the test actually measures.

02

The learners

Adults studying on their own, usually around a full-time job. Many have no aviation background and have never seen a sectional chart or a METAR weather report.

They need short sessions, explanations they can follow without an instructor, and an honest signal of when they're ready to book the test.

03

Design decisions

  • Built a Chart Lab instead of describing charts in textAirspace, obstacles, airport labels and elevation figures are drawn right into the practice questions, so learners practice the visual skill the exam tests.
    Visual learning
  • Organized everything by the test's knowledge areasRegulations, airspace, weather, loading and performance, and operations. Progress maps directly to how the test is scored.
    Blueprint alignment
  • Practice first, explanation alwaysAll 109 questions explain why the right answer is right. Retrieval practice with feedback builds understanding faster than rereading the handbook.
    Retrieval practice
  • Two practice modes for two jobsTopic drills build one skill at a time. A 60-question timed exam in the real format tells learners whether they're ready.
    Assessment design
  • A review queue for missed questionsQuestions a learner gets wrong are saved for review, so study time goes to the gaps instead of what they already know.
    Personalized practice
04

Results

The app is live and free to use. It runs in any browser and saves progress on the learner's own device, so there's no account to create.

05

What's next

  • Add spaced review that brings back missed questions after a few days, not just on demand.
  • Run a usability test with 3–5 first-time learners and revise where they get stuck.
