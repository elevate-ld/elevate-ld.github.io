01

The problem

Security+ is a common entry credential for IT and security jobs. It covers five broad domains, hundreds of acronyms, and performance-based questions that test doing, not just recall.

The biggest problem learners describe isn't the content. It's not knowing whether they're ready, so they either book the exam too early or keep studying long after they need to.

02

The learners

Career changers, help desk staff and veterans moving into IT. Most study at night or in short breaks, often on a phone.

Many feel buried by the vocabulary and need a way to see progress by domain instead of one overall score.

03

Design decisions

  • Mirrored the real exam90 questions weighted by domain, a 90-minute timer and flag-for-review. Practice under real conditions builds confidence and pacing.
    Blueprint alignment
  • Scored by domain, not just overallA domain-by-domain breakdown turns "I got 74%" into "I need to work on risk management," which is a study plan.
    Assessment design
  • Drills modeled on performance-based questionsTap-to-order drills and a ports drill practice the hands-on question types that flashcards alone don't prepare learners for.
    Practice design
  • Feedback that teachesAll 129 questions explain the concept behind the answer, so a wrong answer becomes a short lesson.
    Retrieval practice
  • Short-session study toolsStudy notes for all five domains, flashcards, and a missed-question review queue, all sized for a few minutes at a time.
    Microlearning
04

Results

The app is live and free to use. Progress saves on the learner's own device, so there's no account to create.

05

What's next
